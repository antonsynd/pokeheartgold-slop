# ov96_021FDE7C

Written from the asm, with plain C double and float compares (as in ov96_021FE550) and full prototypes for every
callee (ov96_021FF5A8 takes 6 arguments, ov96_021E8228 takes 5).

The track position at +0x7c wraps at 4194304.0 (the double 0x4150000000000000, 0x400000 in fixed point): past it,
the lap counter at +0x9c goes up; below 0, it goes down. An earlier draft used 768.0 and failed on the trials that
reach the wrap.

The MWCC compare helpers return their result in the flags. Their disassembly confirms that `bl _fgr; bls` is
`!(a > b)`, and that `bl _fleq; bhi` is `!(a <= b)`, NaN included. So plain C compares match.
