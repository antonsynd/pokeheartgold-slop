asm: the callee masks its counter with 0xff), and returns out[(u8)(LCRandom() % n)].

The asm reads out[idx] from its 0x180-byte frame without a bound, and the check's callee stub never fills `out`, so idx
often exceeds 47. The C reproduces the asm's read for the full u8 range: idx < 48 reads `out`; idx 48 is the caller's r4
(read as the function's first statement); idx 49 is lr (`__builtin_return_address(0)`); idx >= 50 reads the caller's frame
at entry sp + 4 * (idx - 50), with entry sp = `__builtin_frame_address(0) + 8` under the check's clang -O0 Thumb build.
In-game (count at most 48) only the first case occurs; the others assume that frame layout.
