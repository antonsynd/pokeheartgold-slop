The two pools of indices live in the original's stack frame and are indexed by a byte that is not bounds checked once
the draws outnumber the pool (counts above 5, or 25 for mode 10), so the loop reads and writes the saved registers
and the caller's frame. The C reproduces that: a helper works on the original's frame at its own addresses (entry
stack pointer minus 0x40), and ov96_021E860C reserves 0x400 bytes of stack and forces clang to save r4-r6 (with r7
and lr) so that they sit where the original's saved registers are. Large counts run past the cycle budget (those
trials agree up to the budget).
