# ov96_021FE550: PASS

Ghidra's draft with its lost double-precision comparisons rewritten from the asm. The draft had dropped the `_dgeq`/`_dgr`
results (the soft-float compare calls become C comparisons on doubles):
- before the loop: `(double)d >= 768.0` selects `(int)(80.0 + (1024.0 - (double)d))`, else `0x50 - d`;
- in the loop: with `base = (s32)puVar3[param_2]` and `cur` the halfword read, `(double)base > 720.0 && cur < 300` gives
  `(int)((1024.0 - base) + (double)cur)`; otherwise `base < 100 && (double)cur > 910.0` gives
  `(int)(0.0 - (base + (1024.0 - cur)))`; the default is `cur - base`; then `+ 0x50`.
The soft-float helpers (`_dflt`, `_dsub`, `_dadd`, `_dfix`, ...) come from clang's double operations. The dead r3 stack-slot
read and the unused callee aliases were removed. Callee arities for the wrappers are in the .arities.json.
