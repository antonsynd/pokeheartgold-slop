original would store anywhere). The original keeps its spilled arguments, the loop counter, counts[4] and the
registers its prologue pushed in one 0x28-byte stack frame; the C models that frame word for word
(`Frame_ov96_02215478`: param_1, param_2, param_3, i, counts[4], saved r4, r5, r6, r7, lr) and resolves
`counts[cell]` to the address `entry sp - 0x18 + cell`: inside the modelled frame when it lands there, otherwise
the real memory at that address. The entry stack pointer is `__builtin_frame_address(0) + 8` under clang -O0 Thumb;
the saved r4-r6 are read from the registers as the first statements, r7 from the frame record and lr from
`__builtin_return_address(0)`. Exact for in-range inputs (cell 0..4) as before. LCRandom is declared u16 (as in HeartGold).
