# ov96_0220F4A0

The asm keeps its locals in a 0x58-byte frame and indexes the 6-word table at sp+0x40 with
`8 * ov96_021E6138(...)` (two words at index 8u-8 and 8u-4) without a bounds check. The game only gives small
u, but the check's stub gives any value, so the original reads past the table into its own saved registers
(r3-r7, lr) and the caller's frame.

The C mirrors the original's frame in `Frame_ov96_0220F4A0` (every slot at the asm's sp offset, including the
outgoing stack arguments the asm leaves behind) and reads the two words through `ov96_0220F4A0_ReadWord`
relative to the entry stack pointer (`__builtin_frame_address(0) + 8` under clang -O0 Thumb): own frame,
then the pushed r3-r7/lr (r3-r6 captured by an inline-asm `stmia` as the first statement, r7 and lr from the
clang frame record), then real memory. For in-range u (1..3) it is simply `buf40[2u-2]`, `buf40[2u-1]`.

Other fixes against the earlier draft: `ov96_021EAA20(r4)` feeds `ov96_021E8BB0(r0)` as the asm chains them,
the draw priority call takes the return value of `ov96_021E64F8`, and arr34/v2c/v30 are left uninitialised as
in the asm (only buf40 is cleared).
