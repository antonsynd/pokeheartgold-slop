Recorded. The asm keeps v0 (4 bytes) at the bottom of its 24-byte frame and indexes it without a bounds check (the
game never passes param1 > 4), so for larger param1 the loops write over the pushed registers and into the caller's
frame at entry_sp - 24 + i. The C reproduces that by using the same absolute array (entry_sp from the frame pointer,
entry_sp - 8). clang's own frame has words in that range (spilled parameters, saved r7 and lr), so the C copies the
parameters to locals first, saves the 24 bytes at entry_sp - 24 and puts them back at the end; guard keeps the other
locals below that range. 1293 of 2000 trials are inconclusive (param1 so large that the original runs out of cycles).
