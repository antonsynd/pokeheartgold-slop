PASS, but about a third of the trials are inconclusive: when the context's active-sprite count is 10 or
more, the original stores a transform result over its own saved lr (it indexes a four-entry stack array with
no bounds check) and never returns, so those trials cannot finish on the original side.

The C models the original's stack frame (the four array entries, then the pushed r3-r7 and lr, then the
caller's stack) so reads past the fourth entry see the same words, and captures the r2/r3 left by the
previous call because the call through `transformFunc` is compared on all four registers.
