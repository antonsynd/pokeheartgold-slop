The asm copies a 3x3x4 s16 table to the stack and indexes it unchecked with the player count at +4 (the game uses 2..4, other counts hit the assert).
Other counts are modelled: past the copy are the pushed r4-r7/lr, then the caller's frame. A count of 1 reads the function's own
offset counter (0) and not-yet-written slots, modelled as 0. 500 of 2000 trials agree only up to the cycle budget (huge counts).
