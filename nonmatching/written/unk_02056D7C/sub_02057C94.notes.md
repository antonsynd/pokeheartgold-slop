# sub_02057C94: PASS

For connectedPlayers <= 4 (the game's maximum) this is the plain grid-matching loop. The asm does not bound
netId / netJd by 4, so with larger counts it indexes past the 2v2 grid and past v6[4] into the rest of its own
frame: v6[4..7] overlay the 2v2 grid (sp+0x20), v6[8..13] overlay the registers the prologue pushed
(r3, r4, r5, r6, r7, lr), and anything further is the caller's frame.

The C models that frame word for word (`frame[14]`: v6 at 0, grid2v2 at 4, saved r3..lr at 8..13) and reaches
the caller's frame through the entry stack pointer (`__builtin_frame_address(0) + 8` under clang -O0 Thumb).
The saved r3..r6 are read from the registers as the function's first statements, the saved r7 from the frame
record and lr from `__builtin_return_address(0)`. Behaviour for in-range inputs is exactly the original's.
The 1v1 grid is only reached with connectedPlayers == 2, so it stays an ordinary local.
