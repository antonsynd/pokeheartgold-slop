- The functions are not `.public` in the asm, but they are non-static here: this part is its own translation unit, and the callers (`sub_02012A90` in another part, the `FadeFunc_*` functions) live elsewhere.
- `sub_02012ACC` fills a byte table: it toggles the fill value once, at the index `start + (end - start) * a2 / a3`, and writes the value for every index from the lower to the upper bound.
