# overlay_75 part 01

28 of the 30 functions of the part pass (ov75_02246960 to ov75_02246E78). Not passing: ov75_02246DFC and ov75_02246E78.

- ov75_02246DFC and ov75_02246E78 call `String_atoi(str, &ok)` and then `GF_ASSERT(ok)`. The asm keeps `ok` in the slot that `push {r3, r4, r5, lr}` reserved, i.e. at [sp], and never writes it itself. check.py stubs the call without writing the out-parameter, so the original reads back the r3 it just pushed (an argument register, nonzero in most trials and zero in some), while the C reads a never-written stack slot, which the sandbox gives as 0. The result is a different number of calls (the assert call) in every trial. The C is the plain `BOOL ok; ... GF_ASSERT(ok);` and matches the asm line for line (ov75_02246DFC reduces the u64 result with `% 10000`, which the asm does through `_ull_mod`, so the cast is `(u64)`; ov75_02246E78 keeps the low word). Initialising `ok` to TRUE passes only the trials where r3 is nonzero; there is no source-level way to put r3 in that slot. Tool limit: the gate cannot model the out-parameter of String_atoi.
- The work struct is 0x11C bytes: +0 child OverlayManager, +4 SaveData, +8 Options, +0xC and +0x10 the buffer and NNS exp heap handle given to the DWC allocator, +0x78 a u16, +0x7A a u8, +0x7C "wireless is up" flag, +0x80 the screen index being run, +0x88 and +0x8C the next screen and a second value, +0x94 the number-input dialog, +0x98 a `Unk020317F4` from the save (the offsets of its unk24 and unk62 fit: ov75_02246CB8 passes +0xBC, ov75_02246CCC writes the halfword at +0xFA), +0x104 and +0x108 two numbers (ov75_02246C68 and ov75_02246C8C save them), +0x10C a result byte, +0x110 and +0x114 two Strings.
- ov75_02249904 is the table of six screens (16 bytes each: setup function, finish function, OverlayManagerTemplate, flag); the data check against the ROM passes with the entries defined here. The templates it points to (ov75_022498F4, ov75_022498E4, _02102620) are declared extern. ov75_02246EAC and ov75_02246EDC, the last entry's functions, belong to the next part.
- `_02249BE0` (the exp heap handle read by the DWC allocator ov75_02246BF0 and the free callback ov75_02246C18) is a bss variable defined here under its address name.
- The overlay ids in ov75_02246B00, ov75_02246B48 and ov75_02246B98 are `FS_OVERLAY_ID(intro_title)` (60) and `FS_OVERLAY_ID(OVY_70)` (70) in the real tree; the gate's linker has no address for the `SDK_OVERLAY_*_ID` symbols, so they are written as the numbers here (`OVY_ID_INTRO_TITLE`, `OVY_ID_70`).
- In ov75_022469D8 the screen's setup function is called before the template is loaded (as in the asm) and the indirect call goes through a local, so that clang leaves r3 alone: check.py compares all four argument registers of an indirect call, and the original leaves r3 as it came in.
- ov75_02246D00 and ov75_02246D04 are the do-nothing setup and finish functions of the first screen; ov75_02246D00 returns its argument (the asm is a bare `bx lr`).
